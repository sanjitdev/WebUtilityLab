WebAnvil · Pre-development Pitch · 2026-08-11

A collection of single-shot browser tools that turn messy data into useful results — without remembering you.

It costs you nothing because it doesn't remember you.

Thesis

A 100-tool site wins on volume. WebAnvil wins on depth: each tool solves a real job end-to-end, runs entirely in the browser, and refuses to pretend it has features competitors can copy. Stateless, no-account, no-history — the absences are the brand.

01

The 5 Truths

  • Stateless by design. Upload → process → download → leave. No history. Section 13's "chainable workflows" is out.
  • Craft, not love. Seven practices: kind errors, graceful edges, teaching empty states, refusing bad jobs, honest changelogs, visible-at-moment-of-trust privacy, public postmortems.
  • No signup. No account. No tracking. Privacy is verifiable in DevTools — zero network calls.
  • 10s budget. Show meaningful result in 5s; pre-flight estimate with tolerance band for longer jobs.
  • One tool at a time. Ship JSON Surgeon excellently. Then assess. Then a second. No parallel bets.

The 1
