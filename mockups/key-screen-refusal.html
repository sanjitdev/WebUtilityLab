Why is there a 10 second budget?

We find that users who wait more than ~12 seconds start to lose trust in the result. A 10 second budget keeps the tool honest about what it can do well in that window — twelve honest seconds rather than eight unexplained seconds. Refusing a job that won’t finish well is part of how we earn trust.
